# Mortuary practice compared with FuneralOS

Sources: South African Regulation R363 (management of human remains, 2013),
municipal health inspection guidance for undertakers' premises, and general
mortuary standard operating procedure (receiving, identification, tagging,
storage, temperature monitoring, release).

| What good practice requires | FuneralOS |
| --- | --- |
| Log arrival time, who brought the body, condition, personal property | Intake records source, collector, condition notes and a belongings register |
| A unique tag on the body, shown on all paperwork | Unique tag number per business, on every screen |
| Unbroken chain of custody: who handled the body, when | Append-only custody events (cannot be edited or deleted); named hand-over and acceptance on every move |
| Surface temperature at or below 5°C within 3 hours of receipt (R363 10(6)(b)) | Cooling deadline per body, alerts when overdue |
| Cold places not over their tray capacity (R363 11(2)(g)) | Capacity per place, warning when nearly full |
| Temperature monitoring with alarms | Reading log per cold place, alert when too warm or when a check is missed |
| Infectious remains travel with a doctor's declaration (R363 12) | Checklist item required before release when flagged infectious |
| Release only to an authorised person with paperwork | Signed family authorisation, recipient name and ID number required |
| Two-person / two-identifier check to prevent mix-ups | Tag number re-typed plus surname or ID read from the body's own tag; refusals are audited |
| Surfaces and equipment cleaned and disinfected immediately after each preparation; protective clothing daily (R363 11(2)) | **New:** cleaning log with six tasks; a preparation with no surfaces and equipment record after it is flagged and the mortuary team is told |
| Unidentified bodies handled promptly | Flagged on the dashboard and alerted daily |
| Hand-off to the next team without phone calls | Operations is notified when remains arrive and when they are released |

Not covered yet (suggested next): scheduled family viewings, post-mortem and
forensic-pathology status, fingerprinting of unidentified remains, and
preparation-room temperature (15°C limit, R363 10(6)(b)).
