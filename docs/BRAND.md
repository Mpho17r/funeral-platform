# LINDA brand

**LINDA** — Linked Intelligence for Networked Development & Administration.
Slogan: *One Platform. Every Business.* Powered by Saccharum Technologies.

- Colours: Deep Navy `#101D2B` (dominant), Warm Gold `#D6A85F` (accent only), White, Slate `#718096`, Light Grey `#F5F6F8`. Functional red/green stay distinct.
- Type: Inter. Logo: geometric "L" mark (`components/LindaLogo.tsx`, `public/linda-icon.svg`).
- Top-right of the app shows the business name with the platform type beneath it: "Funeral Platform" or "Business Management Platform" (from `business_type`).
- Do not market as a funeral-only product. Advertise only industry configurations that are implemented (funeral, general).
- Internal identifiers (package names, repo, DB) are unchanged to protect migrations and compatibility.
